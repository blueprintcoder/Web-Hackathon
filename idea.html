01 Core Vision & Thematic Direction

Standard productivity apps fail because real habits suffer from delayed gratification (hitting the gym or studying takes months to show results). Video games hook players through instant tactile feedback, clear progression curves, and tangible rewards.

To avoid looking like a generic Bootstrap SaaS dashboard (which judges penalize heavily), we adopt the "Solo Leveling / Dark Fantasy Guild" aesthetic.

Theme Vocabulary Immersion
  • Tasks → Bounties & Quests
  • Points → Gold & Mana Crystals
  • Habits → Daily Rites & Streaks
  • Store → Guild Black Market
Alive & Tactile Sensory
  • Audio SFX: Blade chime, coin drops, level fanfare (with mute switch).
  • Spring Physics: Floating +50 XP numbers fly to header.
  • Confetti & Flash: Celebratory level-up modal.
Zero-Lag Feel Performance
  • Optimistic UI: Checkmarks update instantly; backend syncs in background.
  • Skeletons: Smooth loading states.
  • Keyboard: Ctrl+K command palette.

03 RPG Progression Engine & Game Mathematics

The problem statement mandates a non-linear leveling system (each subsequent level requires progressively more XP).

Leveling Formula: Required XP(L) = Math.floor(100 × L1.5) Non-Linear Curve
Level Progression Scale
  • Level 1 → 2: 100 XP (Instant early gratification)
  • Level 2 → 3: 283 XP
  • Level 3 → 4: 520 XP
  • Level 5 → 6: 1,118 XP
  • Level 9 → 10: 2,700 XP (Prestige achievement)

04 Hackathon-Winning "Killer Features"

These features elevate our project beyond standard CRUD submissions into the 1st-place bracket:

1. Weekly Boss Raid: "The Procrastination Demon"

A weekly boss with 3,000 HP (e.g. Malakor the Sloth). Completing any quest deals damage to the boss equal to the quest's XP output plus character Strength bonus. Defeating the boss unlocks rare loot chests and guild badges.

2. Black Market & Real-World Custom Rewards

Users spend earned Gold in two ways:
• Virtual Relics: Scroll of Focus (+10% INT XP), Aegis Shield (Freezes streak if 1 day missed).
• Real-World Rewards: Users create custom rewards (e.g., "1 hr Netflix" = 120 Gold, "Order Pizza" = 400 Gold).

3. Hunter Rank Difficulty Tiers

Quests are categorized by rank:
• E-Rank: Quick (10 XP, 5 Gold)
• C-Rank: Moderate (35 XP, 15 Gold)
• A/S-Rank: Boss Milestones (150+ XP, 80 Gold). Higher stakes motivate bigger efforts.

4. Keyboard Command Palette (Accessibility)

Full accessibility via Cmd/Ctrl+K:
• Press C → Quick New Quest modal
• Press 1/2/3 → Switch tabs (Quests, Boss, Shop)
• Space on highlighted quest checks it off with sound!
